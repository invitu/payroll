This module reconciles the expense reports reimbursed through the
payslip.

When a payslip linked to expense reports is validated, the unreconciled
payable lines of those expense reports are reconciled, on a best-effort
basis, with the matching lines of the payslip accounting move (same
account and same partner).

Configuration
=============

No specific configuration. Map the reimbursement salary rule to the
expense payable account so the payslip move and the expense reports
share the same account and partner; the reconciliation is then
automatic. Otherwise the accountant reconciles manually.
